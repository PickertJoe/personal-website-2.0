Kajabi

As a senior engineer on the Core Product team, I am responsible for designing, leading, and implementing projects that expand and improving the tools that power our users' business.

  • Implementing a white-lableled scheduling service for our coaching product (August 2022)
  • Migrating the entire backend of this service to a new 3rd party provider with zero downtime and zero data loss
  • Creating a Serverless microservice to generate preview screenshots for our website and branded mobile app builders
  • Countless bug fixes and other incremental improvements. Rare is the day when I don't ship multiple PRs
